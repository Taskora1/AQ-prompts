## How Instagram AI Photo Prompts Are Structured

A structured travel-fashion prompt separates four decisions: the adult subject, destination cues, light, and composition. Keeping those parts distinct makes an **Instagram AI photo prompt** easier to customize and troubleshoot.

Location details establish the theme; wardrobe and pose connect the series; lighting defines mood; and the 4:5 direction requests a portrait-oriented composition. None of these instructions guarantees realism, engagement, saves, or accurate identity.

Each prompt includes those elements. Review and adapt the wording, and upload a reference photo only when you own it or have permission to use it.
