## Title
Aesthetic South Asian Male Autumn Park Portrait

## Prompt
A detailed, high-resolution portrait photograph of a confident South Asian man in his late teens (16-18 years old). He is sitting directly forward on a brown-slat wooden park bench, looking straight at the camera. Wearing a deep maroon long-sleeved button-down shirt with rolled-up sleeves and crisp white trousers with a black belt. Accessories: aviator-style sunglasses with silver frames and a silver-toned metal link watch. Right arm rests on his raised left knee. Background: soft-focus, creamy bokeh of an autumn park with warm golden-yellow and green foliage. Lighting: diffused natural daylight. Color grade: warm, inviting, natural skin tones. Composition: medium close-up, centrally focused, shallow depth of field. Shot on 85mm portrait lens, fine-grain digital texture, photorealistic 8K quality.
